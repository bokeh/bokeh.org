02 · Logo usage

Give the mark room to do its work.

A few consistent choices keep the Bokeh logotype legible and recognizable across print and digital applications.

Do
  • Use the primary logo on white or light backgrounds.
  • Use the reversed logo on dark backgrounds.
  • Keep at least 72 px of width for digital use.
  • Leave clear space equal to three times the bar in the “k.”
Do not
  • Stretch, compress, rotate, or redraw the logotype.
  • Change its colors or apply transparency.
  • Place text or other elements over the logo.
  • Use the icon alone to identify the Bokeh project.
